# 🚀 RoRejoinX v1.1.1: The Powerhouse Update!

We are incredibly excited to announce the massive v1.1.1 update. We've completely redesigned the UI to an ultra-modern standard and added four powerhouse features that turn RoRejoinX into the ultimate Roblox utility suite.

## 🔄 Auto-Updating Launcher
* **Persistent Launcher:** The standalone Setup Wizard has been completely re-architected into a persistent, Auto-Updating Launcher that seamlessly installs updates and launches the app in one click.
* **Sleek Launcher UI:** The Auto-Updating Launcher has been redesigned with a borderless glassmorphic loading screen, perfectly centered on your desktop and featuring the official app icon.

## 🎨 Ultra-Modern UI Overhaul
* **Custom Borderless Window:** We stripped away the clunky Windows borders and built a sleek, custom dragable title bar with hover-animated close/minimize buttons.
* **Premium Graphical Icons:** Say goodbye to ugly text-emojis! The sidebar now features beautiful, high-definition transparent PNG icons.
* **Ghost Mode:** The Compact Mini-Widget now becomes 90% transparent when you hover your mouse over it, ensuring it never blocks your gameplay.
* **Setup Wizard:** The standalone Updater app has been completely modernized into a multi-step installation wizard.

## 🔋 Powerhouse Features
* **Eco Mode (FPS Capper):** Save massive amounts of CPU power while AFK farming! When enabled, RoRejoinX actively throttles the Roblox background process to save power and keep your PC cool.
* **Remote Mobile Control:** Remotely stop the watchdog from your phone's browser using the built-in local web server!
* **Auto-Shutdown Scheduler:** Going to sleep? Tell the app to automatically close Roblox, stop watching, and safely shut down your PC at a specific time (e.g., `06:00 AM`).
* **Cloud Profile Sync:** Never lose your saved games again! Securely backup and sync your config directly to the cloud via GitHub Gists.

## ⚙️ Integrations & Improvements
* **Discord Rich Presence (RPC):** Show off your farming stats live on your Discord profile.
* **Audio Alerts:** Added system chimes and sleek slide-in toast notifications for successful crash recoveries.
* **Visual Cards:** Replaced all boring list boxes with interactive visual cards showing live player counts.
